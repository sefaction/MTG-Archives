# Bound correction-library original reads

Related to existing #305 and release tracker #310. This repairs one correction-library memory boundary; both broad issues retain their other criteria and owners.

`readCorrectionBlob` checked pathname size and then read the complete descriptor. An original that grew after that check could exceed its recorded size and the 10 MiB photo limit before being rejected. The corrected reader allocates the validated recorded length plus one byte, handles short reads, and refuses an extra byte or early EOF before hashing. Maximum allocation and consumed bytes are the recorded length plus one, at most 10 MiB plus one. Successful reads return exactly the original bytes. Owner directories, no-follow opening, digest validation and ordered primary/close errors remain in place.

This is distinct from #979's acquisition-photo reader and its unpublished local allocation/upload-body corrections. It does not take over the desktop correction-directory retry-durability work or #958.
